Nature


-by M Nanda kumar

    The sun is shining bright,
    Birds are taking flight.
    Flowers bloom with grace,
    Nature's beauty in every place.

    The gentle breeze does sway,
    Leaves dance and play.
    Rivers flow with cheer,
    Bringing life far and near.

    Mountains stand so tall,
    Majestic, they enthrall.
    In nature's arms we find,
    Peace and joy intertwined.
